# Circuit Breaker

## List of Circuit Breaker

1. <span style="color: red;">**MCB**</span> (Miniature Circuit Breaker)**: Used for final low-current branch circuits like lights and wall plugs (up to 125A).
2. **MCCB** (Molded Case Circuit Breaker): Used for heavy industrial machinery, motors, and sub-panel feeders (16A to 1600A+).
3. **ACB** (Air Circuit Breaker): Used as the primary incoming switch at Main Switchboards directly after a transformer (800A to 6300A).
4. **RCCB** / ELCB (Residual Current Circuit Breaker): A specialized life-safety device that monitors earth leakages to prevent electric shocks.
5. **RCBO** (Residual Current Breaker with Overcurrent): A two-in-one hybrid device that combines the safety functions of an MCB and an RCCB.
6. **MPCB** (Motor Protection Circuit Breaker): Tailored specifically to shield electric motors from overloads and startup current spikes.
7. **VCB** (Vacuum Circuit Breaker): The standard choice for Medium Voltage systems (11kV to 33kV) that safely extinguishes arcs inside a vacuum chamber.
8. **SF6** (Sulfur Hexafluoride Circuit Breaker): Used in High Voltage sub-stations (33kV up to 500kV+) using pressurized gas to quench massive electrical arcs.
9. **OCB** (Oil Circuit Breaker): A legacy high-voltage design where contacts open inside insulating oil (rarely used in modern projects).
10. **Air Blast Circuit Breaker**: An older high-voltage system that shoots high-pressure compressed air to blow out electrical arcs.
11. **AFCI** (Arc Fault Circuit Interrupter): A smart breaker that detects dangerous hidden electrical sparking caused by frayed or damaged wires.
12. **DC Circuit Breaker**: Specially designed to handle continuous Direct Current arcs in Solar PV farms, battery storage, and EV charging grids.
13. **Smart / Digital Circuit Breaker**: An advanced low-voltage breaker equipped with microprocessors to track and send real-time power data to control rooms.
