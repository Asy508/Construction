# Direct Digital Control (DDC)

A direct digital control in Building Management System (BMS) is a computerized brain that automatically manages HVAC unit without human interferance.

## What DDC Does
1. **Read Inputs:** It get data from sensor, such as temperature, pressure & etc.
2. **Run Logic:** It compares the sensor reading with target setting
3. **Drives Output:** It sends signal to change the HVAC unit such as open valve, speed motor & etc.

## How a DDC Setup Looks
1. **Field devices:** Sensor and physical switches in ducts, pipes and rooms. Actuator attach to valve and damper to move them.
2. **DDC Panel:** This metal box mounted at wall near the equipment room.
