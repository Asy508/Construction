# Open Network Video Interface Forum (ONVIF)

ONVIF stands for Open Network Video Interface Forum. In CCTV and security systems, an ONVIF camera means an IP security camera that follows a universal global standard rather than a closed, brand-specific proprietary system

## What ONVIF Does

- Interoperability: It acts like a "universal translator" or common language. It lets you mix and match cameras, Network Video Recorders (NVRs), and Video Management Software (VMS) from different brands (such as connecting an Axis camera to a Hikvision NVR).
- No Vendor Lock-In: You are not forced to buy all your hardware or software from the same manufacturer.
- Core Functions: It standardizes how devices find each other on a network, authenticate users, handle PTZ (pan-tilt-zoom) controls, and stream video.

## ONVIF Profiles Table

|Profile	|Focus Area	Key Features & Purpose	|Current Status|
|--- |--- |---|
|Profile S	|Video Surveillance (Basic)	Basic video streaming, H.264 support, PTZ control, and relay outputs.	|Ending support (March 31, 2027)|
|Profile T	|Video Surveillance (Advanced)	Advanced video streaming, H.264/H.265 compression, imaging settings, motion/audio events, and security enhancements.	|Current (Replacement for Profile S)|
|Profile G	|Edge Recording & Storage	Storage, search, retrieval, and playback of video data from edge devices (such as NVRs or cameras with local memory).	|Current|
|Profile M	|Metadata & Analytics	Unified streaming of analytics data, object classification, and metadata from AI/video analytics engines.	|Current|
|Profile A	|Access Control Configuration	Access control client and device configuration, user credentials, and badge management.	|Current|
|Profile C	|Access Control Door Events	Physical access control integration, door status, lock control, and event handling.	Current|
|Profile D	|Access Control Peripherals	Peripheral input/output devices, such as locks, readers, and biometric devices.	|Current|
|Profile V	|Cloud Video	Cloud-based video surveillance and management workflows.	|Release Candidate|
|Profile Q	|Quick Installation	Simplified out-of-the-box discovery and configuration.	|Deprecated (April 1, 2022)|
