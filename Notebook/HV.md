## Distribution Panel

1. Floor-standing power distribution (5cm base height using channel steel)
2. Wall-mounted lightning and socket distribution


- A distribution panel is an equipment used to centralize, switch, and distribute electrical power. It typically consists of a cabinet, switches (circuit breakers), protective devices, monitoring instruments, energy meters, and other secondary components. Distribution panels are usually installed in power stations, substations, areas with high electricity consumption, and near major electrical equipment
- At the early stage of distribution panel fabrication, the installation site’s available space should be initially assessed. Based on the internal components and actual site conditions, the panel size must be confirmed with the manufacturer. If the site does not meet the required dimensions, the panel size needs to be reconsidered.
