## Installation Method & Workflows

1. Supply water installation
2. Drainage water installation
3. Pipe connection
    1. Threaded
    2. Welding
    3. Socket
    4. Adhesive
    5. Flange
    6. Coupling
    7. Groove coupling
    8. Ferrule
    9. Heat fusion
    10. Electrofusion
    11. Press fitting
    12. Heat shrink tube

## Inspection & Testing

1. Hydrostatic test (All pressure pipeline system)
2. Water-filling test (All non-pressure system)
3. Ball passing test (Vertial and horizontal main pipe: ball size > 2/3 pipe)

### Concealed/Buried pipeline
1. Must be test before buried
2. Fill the pipe for 15minutes
3. IF drop fill again for 5 minutes

### Water tank
1. water test: fill up tank to stand 24H
2. hydrostatic test: test pressure stand 10 minutes

### Vertical drainage
1. Insert ball from the top
2. Flush water to push ball out

### Horizontal main/outlet pipes
1. Insert ball at upstream
2. Flush water to push ball throught the outlet

## Quality Management 

1. Pipe must be vertical and horizontal
2. Thread - 2 or 3 thread must be exposed and excess hemp fiber
3. Flanged - Size,washer
4. Welds -  smooth and clean
5. Support & Hangers   
6. Labeling
