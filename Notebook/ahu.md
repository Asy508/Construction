
# Air Handling Unit (AHU)

1. Air Intake & Mixing Section

- Damper: Motorized metal blades or shutters that open and close to control the ratio of fresh outdoor air and recycled return air entering the AHU.
- Mixing Box / Mixing Chamber: The enclosed section where fresh air from outside blends with return air from the building before filtration.

2. Air Filtration Section (The Cleaners)

• Pre-Filter: A coarse, pleated mesh filter designed to trap large particles like dust bunnies, hair, and bugs. It protects the more expensive filters down the line.
• Bag Filter / Pocket Filter: A secondary, high-efficiency fabric filter that captures medium-sized particles like pollen, mold spores, and fine dust.
• HEPA Filter: High-Efficiency Particulate Air filters, usually installed only in cleanrooms, labs, or hospitals to trap microscopic bacteria and viruses.
• Filter Frame & Pressure Gauge (Magnehelic Gauge): The housing that holds the filters airtight, equipped with a dial gauge that measures how dirty the filters are by calculating the pressure drop across them.

3. Thermal Conditioning Section (The Chilled Water System)

• Cooling Coil (Chilled Water Coil): A grid of copper tubes with thin aluminum fins wrapped around them. Ice-cold Chilled Water Supply (CHWS) flows inside these tubes to cool the air blowing over them.
• Heating Coil: Similar to the cooling coil, but uses hot water, steam, or electric strip elements to heat the air during cold weather.
• Drain Pan / Condensate Pan: A stainless steel tray sitting directly beneath the cooling coil. When warm air hits the ice-cold coil, it "sweats" (condenses), and this pan catches the water droplets so they can be piped away.

4. Humidity Control Section (Optional)

• Humidifier: Steam nozzles or water-spray manifolds used to inject moisture into the air stream if the building air becomes too dry.
• Dehumidifier: Usually achieved naturally by the cold cooling coil, but some units have dedicated desiccant wheels or reheat coils to strip extra moisture from the air.

5. Moving and Silencing Section

• Blower / Fan (Centrifugal or Plug Fan): The heavy-duty heart of the AHU that physically pulls air through the filters/coils and pushes it into the building.
• Fan Motor & Drive Assembly: The electric motor that powers the fan, connected either directly (Direct Drive) or via pulleys and rubber belts (Belt Drive).
• Vibration Isolators: Heavy-duty steel springs or rubber pads underneath the motor and fan housing to absorb mechanical shaking and prevent the building's structure from vibrating.
• Silencer / Sound Attenuator: Acoustic baffle panels lined with fiberglass insulation placed near the outlet to muffle the roaring sound of the fan.

6. Casing & Internal Support

• Double-Skin Panel Casing: The insulated outer metal box. It uses a "sandwich" of two sheet metal skins with insulation in the middle to prevent cold air from escaping and the box from sweating.
• Access Doors & Inspection Windows: Airtight, hinged doors with rubber gaskets that allow maintenance workers to walk inside the AHU to change filters or fix motors.
