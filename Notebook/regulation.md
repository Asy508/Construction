# Regulation

## [Electricity Regulation 1994](../Electricity-Regulations-1994.pdf)

• Competent Persons & Certification: Anyone performing specialized electrical work—such as Competent Electrical Engineers, Electrical Supervisors, Chargemen, Wiremen, and Cable Jointers—must hold a valid Certificate of Competency issued by the commission.
• Installation & Registration: Electrical installations must meet specific safety, material, and design standards, and certain high-capacity or commercial installations require official registration.
• Safety Standards: Enforces strict safety rules, including proper earthing, residual current devices (RCDs), safe clearances for aerial power lines, and mandatory warning notices.
• Equipment Approval: Electrical equipment manufactured, imported, or sold in Malaysia must comply with regulatory controls and obtain a Certificate of Approval (CoA) or Certificate of Registration (CoR).
• Inspection and Testing: Regular testing and inspections of electrical systems and substations are required to ensure public safety and operational reliability.
