
# Your 5-Day ELV Learning Roadmap

## Day 1: ELV Fundamentals & Safety
	• Learn what ELV means (typically operating at below 50V AC or 120V DC ripple-free).
	• Understand the core purpose: ELV systems handle data, control, and communication, whereas High Voltage (HV/LV) handles raw power.
	• Study basic site safety, cable types (Cat6, Fiber, Coaxial, Twisted Pair), and containment systems (conduits, trunking, cable trays).
## Day 2: Life Safety Systems (Fire Alarm & Public Address)
	• Build on what you learned yesterday about Conventional vs. Addressable Fire Alarm Systems and Single-Line Circuits (SLC).
	• Learn about Public Address and Voice Alarm (PAVA) systems used for emergency announcements and background music.
	• Understand how the Fire Alarm panel triggers the PAVA and cuts off the building's mechanical ventilation during an emergency.
## Day 3: Security & Access Control Systems (SMS)
	• Study CCTV (Closed-Circuit Television): Learn the difference between older Analog systems and modern IP (Internet Protocol) cameras, NVRs (Network Video Recorders), and PoE (Power over Ethernet) switches.
	• Study Access Control Systems (ACS): Learn how card readers, electromagnetic locks (EM locks), break-glasses, and door controllers talk to each other.
## Day 4: Communication & Telecommunication Systems
	• Study Structured Cabling: Learn about the Main Distribution Frame (MDF) room, Intermediate Distribution Frame (IDF) closets, and patch panels.
	• Understand Intercom systems (audio/video door entry) and Master Antenna TV (MATV/SMATV) for satellite signals.
## Day 5: Building Automation & Integration (BMS)
	• Learn about the BMS (Building Management System) or Smart Home systems.
	• Understand how ELV integration allows different systems to talk to each other (e.g., a card reader unlocking a door while telling the CCTV camera to record, and the BMS turning on the lights in that room).

# Adjusted 5-Day Roadmap

## Day 1: Construction Workflow & The ELV Department
	• Learn how a project moves from Design to Site Construction.
	• Map out the ELV team's role and locate your primary guide: the Weak Current System Manual.
## Day 2: Core ELV Systems (Weak Current)
	• Study Fire Alarm Systems, Addressable vs. Conventional loops, and Single-Line Circuits (SLC).
## Day 3: Architectural Integration (Clean Room & Floors)
	• Study the Clean Room Partition and Raised Floor manuals to understand where ELV devices and cables are physically mounted.
## Day 4: Structural Protection & Heavy MEP Coordination
	• Study Fireproof Sealing and HV/HV Electrical manuals to learn about fire stopping and avoiding electrical interference.
## Day 5: Mechanical & Piping Coordination
	• Study Air Conditioning Ductwork and Process Piping manuals to learn how to route ELV trays around massive utilities.
